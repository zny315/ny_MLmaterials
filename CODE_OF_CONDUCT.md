# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free, and constructive project
environment.

Contributors should use welcoming language, critique ideas rather than people,
respect privacy and attribution, and avoid discriminatory or disruptive
behavior. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their
GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
